## 7.1. NISAR MISSION: PUBLIC RELEASE OF S-BAND DATA

**Why in News?**
ISRO began public release of S-Band radar data from the NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar (NISAR) mission through its Bhoonidhi Earth observation portal in late July 2026, marking a key transition to the mission's science phase.

**About NISAR**
- **Joint Mission:** NISAR is a collaborative mission between NASA and ISRO, launched on 30 July 2025 from Satish Dhawan Space Centre using GSLV-F16.
- **Dual-Frequency Radar:** It is the first spaceborne radar to carry both L-band and S-band frequencies on a single satellite, enabling all-weather, day-and-night imaging.
- **Wide Coverage:** Equipped with a 12-metre reflector antenna and SweepSAR technique, it captures swaths of approximately 240 km with high resolution.
- **Orbit & Revisit:** Orbiting at 747 km in a Sun-synchronous orbit, NISAR revisits the same location every 12 days.
- **Data Availability:** Starting with data from Cycle 25 (8 July 2026 onwards), S-band data is being made available on Bhoonidhi as it is processed. Older data will be reprocessed and added in phases.

**Significance**
- **Disaster Monitoring:** Enables tracking of floods, landslides, coastal erosion, and other rapidly changing events regardless of cloud cover or daylight.
- **Resource Management:** Supports precision agriculture, irrigation mapping, wetland monitoring, and coastal zone planning.
- **Cryosphere & Geophysics:** Facilitates monitoring of glacier movement, ice sheets, sea ice, and subtle surface shifts (subsidence).
- **Open Access:** Promotes broader scientific research and applications by making calibrated radar data publicly accessible.

**Way Forward**
- **Calibration Improvements:** Addressing known minor striping over featureless areas and occasional radio frequency interference through ongoing updates.
- **Expanded Applications:** Continued validation through ISRO's nationwide network of ground-based reflectors to enhance data accuracy for diverse user needs.

*Source: ISRO, Times of India, The New Indian Express, 27-28 Jul 2026*

## 7.2. DRDO ADVANCES INDIGENOUS MISSILE AND JET ENGINE CAPABILITIES

**Why in News?**
DRDO conducted the maiden flight-test of the 'Kusha' Long-Range Surface-to-Air Missile on 23 July 2026 and, on 22 July 2026, received the first indigenously developed 350 kg thrust-class expendable turbojet engine from industry partner Azad Engineering.

**About Kusha Missile System**
- **Maiden Test:** Successfully intercepted a high-speed, high-altitude electronic target off the coast of Odisha on 23 July 2026.
- **Capabilities:** Designed to neutralise a wide range of aerial threats including fighter jets, missiles, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), and large aircraft across varied ranges and altitudes.
- **Indigenous Development:** All major elements—missiles, radars, and command & control centre—have been developed by DRDO laboratories in collaboration with industry partners.

**About Indigenous Expendable Turbojet Engine**
- **Development:** Designed by DRDO's Gas Turbine Research Establishment (GTRE), the 350 kg thrust-class expendable turbojet engine was realised and delivered by Azad Engineering, Hyderabad, on 22 July 2026.
- **Technological Significance:** Jet engine development remains among the most complex aerospace technologies, mastered by only a handful of nations.

**Significance**
- **Strategic Autonomy:** Reduces import dependence in critical air defence and propulsion technologies.
- **Defence Industrial Base:** Demonstrates growing maturity of India's public-private partnership model in high-precision defence manufacturing.
- **Ecosystem Boost:** Strengthens capabilities across design, metallurgy, advanced manufacturing, and systems integration.

*Source: PIB, DRDO, 23 Jul 2026*

## 7.3. INDIA'S FIRST DENGUE VACCINE GETS REGULATORY APPROVAL

**Why in News?**
The Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) granted marketing authorisation in July 2026 for Qdenga®, India's first approved Dengue Tetravalent Vaccine (Live, Attenuated), for individuals aged 4 to 60 years.

**About Qdenga®**

| Feature | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Manufacturer/Importer | Takeda GmbH, Germany; imported by Takeda Biopharmaceuticals India Pvt. Ltd. |
| Technology | Live, attenuated tetravalent vaccine using recombinant DNA technology; produced in Vero cells with DENV-2 backbone. |
| Indication | Prevention of dengue disease in individuals aged 4–60 years. |
| Schedule | Two doses (0.5 ml each) subcutaneously, administered at 0 and 3 months. |
| Regulatory Status | Approved in 42 countries; WHO prequalified; over 24 million doses distributed globally. |

**Significance**
- **Public Health Impact:** Comes amid rising dengue burden in India, with cases increasing substantially over the past two decades.
- **Broad Coverage:** Protects against all four dengue serotypes (DENV-1–4), can be given regardless of prior dengue exposure, and does not require pre-vaccination testing.
- **Evidence-Based Approval:** Supported by global Phase III trials (including TIDES/DEN-301) and a Phase III study in the Indian population, demonstrating sustained efficacy and favourable safety profile.
- **Complementary Tool:** Expected to supplement existing measures such as vector control, surveillance, early diagnosis, and case management under the National Vector Borne Disease Control Programme.

*Source: PIB, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, The Hindu, The Indian Express, 20-21 Jul 2026*

## 7.4. NEWS IN SHORTS

### 7.4.1. ISRO TARGETS SEVEN LAUNCHES IN FY 2026-27
- **Launch Plan:** ISRO Chairman V. Narayanan announced a target of at least seven launches in the current financial year, with the next mission expected within two months.
- **Infrastructure:** Plans to commission the second launch complex at Kulasekarapattinam within the financial year to support higher launch frequency.
- **Context:** Announcement followed the successful maiden orbital launch of Skyroot Aerospace's Vikram-1 rocket.
- *Source: The Economic Times, ISRO, 18 Jul 2026*

### 7.4.2. DEPARTMENT OF SPACE TIGHTENS EXIT NORMS FOR KEY MISSIONS
- **New Directive:** DoS issued an internal memorandum on 14 July 2026 restricting routine acceptance of voluntary retirement/resignation requests from Group A scientific/technical personnel working on Gaganyaan and other critical missions.
- **Centralised Approval:** Such requests must now be referred to DoS with centre directors' recommendations, reversing a 2020 delegation of powers.
- **Rationale:** Aimed at safeguarding continuity of nationally important projects amid reported attrition.
- *Source: The Hindu, The Economic Times, Times of India, 15-16 Jul 2026*

### 7.4.3. GAGANYAAN TEST FLIGHT TIMELINE INDICATED FOR 2027
- **Timeline Signal:** ISRO Chairman indicated the first uncrewed test flight for Gaganyaan could slip to Q3 2027, while noting efforts to meet an earlier timeline.
- **Priority on Safety:** Emphasis on crew escape systems, human-centric systems, and overall mission readiness before crewed flights.
- *Source: The Indian Express, 28 Jun 2026*

### 7.4.4. INDIGENOUS CRYSTAL TOXIN GENE TO COMBAT PINK BOLLWORM
- **Discovery:** ICAR-Sugarcane Breeding Institute (SBI), Coimbatore, isolated a novel Bacillus thuringiensis crystal toxin gene effective against pink bollworm resistant to Bollgard II cotton.
- **Commercialisation:** MoU signed with Rasi Seeds to develop transgenic cotton hybrids, with commercial seeds expected around 2030 under a Public-Private Partnership.
- **Significance:** Addresses a major threat to cotton sustainability and farmer livelihoods.
- *Source: PIB, The Hindu, 16 Jul 2026*

### 7.4.5. ICMR TRANSFERS THREE INDIGENOUS BIOMEDICAL TECHNOLOGIES
- **SHetA2:** Licensed to Emcure Pharmaceuticals; a novel anti-HPV therapeutic candidate for treatment of Cervical Intraepithelial Neoplasia (CIN), with potential for safe, affordable intervention.
- **Typhoid Vaccine Candidate:** Fusion construct of Salmonella typhi outer membrane protein, licensed to Biological E Ltd.
- **Broad Enteric Vaccine:** Recombinant composition targeting Salmonella typhi/paratyphi and Shigella, licensed to Biological E Ltd.
- **Platform:** Facilitated under ICMR's Medical Innovations Patent Mitra initiative to accelerate translation of public-funded research.
- *Source: PIB, 21 Jul 2026*
